Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 · Last updated 9 September 2026 · Version 1.0

In short

We do not sell, rent or monetise your health data. Everything Blushy knows about your health is something you entered yourself. You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time.

1. Who we are

Blushy is operated by Blushy Healthcare Private Limited, incorporated in India. This policy covers the Blushy mobile application and this website.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account — an email address and password, or Google sign-in. Passwords are stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash and cannot be read by us.
  • Profile — a name of your choosing and date of birth; optionally a photo and phone number.
  • Health entries — periods, cycle length, symptoms, pain, mood, sleep, hydration, activity, stress and medication adherence. All self-reported.
  • Reproductive and life-stage information you choose to provide, including pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause and menopause.
  • Journal entries, written or spoken.
  • Conversations with our AI assistant, Docsy.
  • Uploads — community and message images, partner attachments, and medical reports.
  • Community content — posts, comments and direct messages.

Collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, app version and error logs.
  • Session tokens and login timestamps.
  • IP address, as with any internet request.

What we do not collect

  • No location. Blushy never requests location permission on any platform.
  • No advertising. No ads, no ad networks, no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics or similar. Usage counts live in our own database against a pseudonymous identifier and never carry your health entries.
  • No payments. Blushy processes no payments and holds no card or bank details.
  • No device or wearable data. We do not connect to Google Fit, Apple Health or any tracker.
  • No doctor consultations. Blushy does not connect you to clinicians and transmits nothing to any clinic or insurer.

3. Voice recordings

Audio you record is sent to our transcription provider, held in memory for that request only, and discarded once the text is produced. The audio is never written to disk or stored in our database — only the transcript is kept, as part of the entry you created. Voice notes you send to a partner are the exception: those are stored as files so the recipient can play them.

4. Who processes your data

This is the complete list of third parties acting on our behalf.

  • Render — application hosting, Singapore.
  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting.
  • Brevo — verification and service email, EU.
  • OpenRouter — the AI assistant and generated notes.
  • Groq — speech-to-text transcription.
  • Google — only if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • An S3-compatible storage provider, for uploaded files.

Your data is processed outside India. Our servers are in Singapore, email delivery is in the EU, and AI requests go to providers outside India. Using Blushy means your data crosses borders.

Whether the AI providers retain or train on what they receive is governed by their own terms. We do not use your data to train any model of our own.

5. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS. Cleartext HTTP is disabled.
  • Stored data is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Passwords are bcrypt-hashed and cannot be recovered or displayed by us.
  • Audit and analytics records store that an event happened, never the health content behind it.

Blushy is not end-to-end encrypted

Our servers read the health data you log. They have to: calculating your cycle, finding patterns and answering you through Docsy all happen on the server. We do not sell it, and nothing reaches a partner unless you switch that sharing on.

6. Partner Mode

Partner Mode is optional and off by default. When a connection is created, every sharing permission starts switched off. A partner sees only the categories you individually enable, and you can switch any of them off again at any time.

7. Your consent

We process your health data because you agreed to it, and we keep a record of that agreement — which documents you accepted, which version of each, and when. We ask before you enter any health information, not after. If we change this policy materially we will ask again rather than assume your earlier agreement covers new wording. You can withdraw your agreement in the app; withdrawing stops us relying on it and the health features stop working, but it deletes nothing. To remove your data, delete your account.

8. Your rights and retention

You may ask for access to your data, correction of it, or its deletion. Most of it can be edited directly in the app. We keep your information while your account exists and delete it when you delete the account. Email verification codes are deleted automatically 30 minutes after they are created.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@blushy.life.

9. Account deletion

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, or through the account deletion page. Deletion is permanent.

10. Children

Blushy is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and will delete it promptly if we become aware of it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes are notified in the app before they take effect, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: info@blushy.life.
