Delete your Blushy account

Effective 9 September 2026 · Last updated 9 September 2026 · Version 1.0

You can permanently delete your Blushy account and everything stored against it. There are two ways to do it, and neither costs anything.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Blushy and go to My Health.
  2. Scroll to Delete Account Permanently.
  3. Confirm, then type DELETE when asked.

Your account is removed straight away. You do not need to email us.

Option 2 — by email

If you no longer have the app installed, write to us from the address your account is registered to.

Email a deletion request

We verify that the request comes from the registered address before acting on it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, login credentials and profile.
  • Period, cycle, symptom, pain, mood, sleep and lifestyle logs.
  • Onboarding answers and life-stage information.
  • Journal entries, including voice transcripts.
  • Docsy conversation history and the summaries made from it.
  • Partner connections, sharing permissions and content shared through them.
  • Community posts, comments and direct messages.
  • Uploaded images, attachments and medical reports.
  • Notification, reminder and analytics records tied to your account.

Deletion is permanent

Once deleted, your data cannot be restored. If you only want us to stop relying on your consent without losing your entries, use withdraw consent in the app instead.

What may be kept

We may retain a limited amount of information where the law requires it — for example in connection with an active legal or regulatory matter. Anything retained is limited to what is legally necessary and handled under the safeguards in our Privacy Policy.
