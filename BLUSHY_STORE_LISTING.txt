FULL DESCRIPTION (2622 / 4000)
==============================================
Blushy is a women's health companion that follows the stage of life you are actually in — not one generic cycle screen for everyone.

WHERE YOU ARE, NOT WHERE AVERAGE IS
Choose the stage that fits, and the whole app changes with it:
• Before your first period
• After your first period
• Living with your cycle
• Hormonal health — PCOS, endometriosis, PMDD, thyroid
• Trying to conceive
• Pregnancy
• Postpartum
• Perimenopause
• Menopause
• Everyday wellness

What you are asked to log, what you are shown, and what Docsy talks about all follow that choice. Your stage can change, and the app changes with you.

LOG WHAT MATTERS, IN ONE PLACE
One logging sheet, stage-aware: symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, pain, flow, cravings, weight and basal temperature. A menopause day asks about hot flushes; a postpartum day asks about feeding and recovery. Nothing irrelevant, nothing missing.

DOCSY, YOUR AI COMPANION
Ask a question in your own words and get a considered answer, not a search result. Docsy sees what you have actually logged, so the reply is about your week rather than a generic article — and it answers in your language.

PATTERNS, HONESTLY
Blushy shows what changed and what stayed steady across your entries. When there is not enough data to say something useful, it says so instead of inventing a number. No false precision about your body.

A PARTNER PORTAL YOU CONTROL
Invite a partner by email. Then decide, permission by permission, what they can see — thirteen separate switches, and nothing is shared until you turn one on. Change your mind at any time; it takes effect immediately.

Together you get a private space: messages, shared activities, small gestures, and Argument Mode for the days when you would rather pause sharing than explain yourself. Either of you can disconnect at any time, and the other is told.

M STUDIO
• Journal — write, search and revisit
• Recovery — rest and recharge
• Time Capsules — write now, open later
• Bouquet — send a postcard and a flower bloom

COMMUNITY
Ask what you would not ask aloud. Moderated discussions with people in the same stage.

SEVEN LANGUAGES
English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, ಕನ್ನಡ, தமிழ் and తెలుగు.

YOUR DATA
• Nothing is shared with a partner until you say so
• Every sharing permission is separate, and reversible
• Delete your account and everything in it from inside the app
• Your health data is never sold and is never used for advertising

Blushy supports your own understanding of your body. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a clinician. If something worries you, please speak to a doctor.

Blushy is for adults aged 18 and over.
